## 1. The full story, in order (verified, with sources)

### Act 1 - The idea: an album as a Renaissance art object (2006-2014)
- **Who:** Tarik "Cilvaringz" Azzougarh, a Dutch-Moroccan Wu-Tang superfan turned affiliate producer, made the beats; RZA was executive producer / co-architect. (Sundance profile of *The Disciple*; Complex, Mar 2015.)
- **Recording:** secret, over roughly six years (sources range 5-9 years); sessions in the US/Europe (Staten Island per NYT via Wikipedia; Sundance says "across Europe"), produced/finished in Marrakech, Morocco. Guests reportedly include all members, Redman, Cher (two appearances), Carice van Houten, FC Barcelona players. Method Man later said members were not told it was a Wu-Tang album (Vanity Fair, Jul 2024; AV Club).
- **Announcement:** Forbes (Zack O'Malley Greenburg), March 26, 2014: "Why Wu-Tang Will Release Just One Copy Of Its Secret Album" https://www.forbes.com/sites/zackomalleygreenburg/2014/03/26/why-wu-tang-will-release-just-one-copy-of-its-secret-album/ (Forbes blocked our fetcher; content confirmed via TIME and others).
- **RZA (TIME, Mar 26, 2014, https://time.com/39243/wu-tang-clan-secret-album-single-copy):** "We're making a single-sale collector's item." / "This is like somebody having the scepter of an Egyptian king."
- **Cilvaringz (same TIME piece):** "One leak of this thing nullifies the entire concept." (Irony: the owner later leaked it.)
- **The art-world argument:** streaming and piracy had reduced music's value; album site text (cited by the Guardian, Mar 27, 2014): "The music industry is in crisis. The intrinsic value of music has been reduced to zero." Framed as a return to Renaissance-style patronage: one patron owns the work, like a painting. RZA (Sundance blog, 2026): "The idea of music being art — of music being a work of art and a piece of art — that's what this is all about." RZA reportedly compared it to the Mona Lisa (Grantland, Mar 2015, paraphrase).
- **Original plan (2014):** 31 tracks, ~128 minutes (TIME), one copy, to **tour museums, galleries and festivals** where visitors would pay to listen under heavy security, THEN be sold to the highest bidder. The tour never happened; only the PS1 preview took place before the sale.
- **The vault:** kept in a vault in Marrakech (Forbes 2014; Royal Mansour hotel per a Wikipedia mirror; another mirror says Casablanca - conflicting). Forbes reporters flew to Marrakech in May 2014 and heard a 51-second snippet ("Unlocking The Wu-Tang Clan's Secret Album In Morocco", May 6, 2014).
- **Offers:** RZA said bids reached **$5 million** (Rolling Stone / Billboard, spring 2014) - a claim, never a sale.
- **Fan reaction:** April 2014 two fans launched a Kickstarter to raise **$5M** to buy and release it free; it failed (Kicktraq "Funding Unsuccessful"; FACT, Apr 18, 2014).
- **The box:** hand-carved nickel-silver box(es) inside a leather/cedar box, designed by **British-Moroccan artist Yahya** (CONFIRMED in PleasrDAO's 2024 verified complaint, para 15). Plus a 174-page leather-bound manuscript (lyrics, credits, anecdotes; FOIA coverage says 175 pages), gold-leafed certificate of authenticity, customized speakers (reported $55,000 - secondary sources only), wax Wu-Tang seal. Artist Jason Koza sued in Feb 2016 over alleged unauthorized use of his artwork in the book; withdrew May 2016 (Hollywood Reporter / Reuters via Wikipedia).

## 2. The 88-year restriction - what it actually says

**Primary wording we could access** (PleasrDAO verified complaint, June 10, 2024, para 20-22, quoting the Purchase Agreement of Sept 3, 2015):
> prohibiting Shkreli, "for eighty-eight years following the sale, from duplicating, replicating, or exploiting the Album for any reason other than for 'exhibition or playing,' in 'spaces not customarily used as venues for large musical concerts,' or advertising or promoting the same."
- If the owner earns "net profits" from exhibiting/playing it, the Producers (RZA, Cilvaringz) get a share.
- Resale only "under the same terms and conditions"; any buyer must be bound "by written agreement to the same rights, restrictions, and obligations on the use, sale, and transfer of the Work."
- The complaint's paraphrase mixes quoted fragments with its own words; the full contract is not public (FOIA copy was redacted).

**What this means in plain words:**
- **Exhibitions / listening events: ALLOWED**, but only in small, non-concert venues (homes, galleries, museums). That is why MONA was legal and why there are "listening sessions" rather than concerts.
- **Commercial release (sale, streaming for money): NOT allowed for 88 years** -> 88 years from 2015 = **2103**.
- **Free release?** 2015 press (Paddle8/Forbes, Mar 2015 "Wu-Tang Clan Set To Release Secret Album--In 88 Years, Give Or Take"; Wikipedia summary) said the buyer could **release it for free** or play it at listening parties, just not commercially exploit it. The complaint's wording ("duplicating, replicating, or exploiting ... for any reason other than exhibition or playing") reads stricter. **CONFLICT - unverified which is right**; safest on-air phrasing: "nobody can sell it until 2103."
- **Copyright split (per Shkreli's 2026 filing):** 50% of copyrights to the buyer in 2015, the other 50% to be transferred in 2103. PleasrDAO says it bought that remaining half from RZA/Cilvaringz in Jan 2024. This is the core of the current dispute.
- **NFT twist:** PleasrDAO's $1 NFT "decrypts" in 2103, each sale moving the date 88 seconds earlier (Dec 5, 2102 as of July 2025).
- **MONA visitors' contract:** recording ban obligations "continue for the remainder of your life or until 2103" (BBC first-person report, via MyJoyOnline, Jun 17, 2024).

## 2b. MONA (Hobart, Tasmania), June 15-24, 2024 - exactly what visitors got

## 4. The strangest TRUE details (ranked as hooks)
1. **The government had to decide what a work of art is worth - and called its price a "trade secret".** The DOJ sold it for $2,238,482.30 to a Hong Kong shell, hid the price for four years, and a judge later ruled the album itself can be a trade secret - the first artwork treated like Coca-Cola's recipe. (Bloomberg FOIA 2025; Chen ruling Sept 2025)
2. **To hear it in Tasmania you signed a contract binding you "for the remainder of your life or until 2103"**, then a man in gloves played the CD on a PlayStation One and locked it back in a safe. (BBC, Jun 2024)
3. **The "only copy" has MP3 copies.** The buyer said on livestream "of course I made MP3 copies, they're like hidden in safes all around the world" and streamed it to 4.9K people on X. (Court complaint)
4. **Every $1 NFT sold moves the release date 88 seconds closer** - by 2025 it had crept to Dec 5, 2102. (Stereogum; DMN)
- Runners-up: the buyer threatened to erase Ghostface's verses; the box set off US customs alarms and Cilvaringz was held 3 hours; Method Man said members didn't know they were recording a Wu-Tang album; the producer once said "One leak of this thing nullifies the entire concept"; the government photos show the priceless box scratched, tarnished and covered in fingerprints; Bill Murray's "heist clause" never existed.
