# Cool Culture Club: material research for the 21 videos (working file, 2026-11-16)

One place for everything each video needs on screen, so we can research and download material and edit as we go.
Scripts are copied from `SCRIPTS_20.md` (the master: edit lines there first, then here). Research excerpts are
copied from `investigacion/` (full text there). Nothing downloaded yet.

## How to use
- **Shot list:** fill "Visual needed" and "Source / file" for every B line. A lines are the head (Kling): no material.
- **Download list:** tick each item as it moves along.
- **Downloads:** save files in `ccc/material/<ID>_<name>/`, in the subfolders `works`, `footage` and `photos`. Keep the
  original file name and add a `.txt` next to it with the URL, licence and credit line.
- **Status legend:** ☐ to find · 🔎 found, licence to check · ⬇ downloaded · ✅ cleared for use · ⛔ licence needed / do not use
- **Rights level:** GREEN = public domain, download freely · AMBER = PD art plus copyrighted footage (short, credited
  commentary excerpts or a licence) · RED = the main visual is in copyright or blocked.
- **Rules:** never use agency footage (Reuters, AP, Getty, AFP) without a licence. Never use any Wu-Tang album audio.
  Download from Commons or open museum sites (The Met, Getty, Rijksmuseum, Nasjonalmuseet), never from Google Images.

### Portrait
**Portrait:** No painted portrait is widely used; the alabaster *Self-portrait* (Bode-Museum) serves. Commons photo is a user photo, check licence (CC BY-SA likely).

**Quote (about Messerschmidt, by Friedrich Nicolai, who visited him in 1781):**
> "A bed, a flute, a tobacco-pipe,
> and an Italian book about human proportions"
> (his entire furniture)
> ... and "the sixty-four different varieties of grimace."

Source: Friedrich Nicolai, *Beschreibung einer Reise durch Deutschland und die Schweiz im Jahre 1781*, vol. 6 (Berlin and Stettin, 1785); English as quoted in JSTOR Daily, "The Man Whose Face Got Stuck Like That", and The Paris Review blog (2010). Lines are fragments; present them as fragments.

### Copyright
**Copyright:** The sculptures are PD (died 1783). Photos of 3D works are new photographic works: use Met (CC0), Getty Open Content, Belvedere (CC BY-SA 4.0, credit required) or Commons files with explicit free licences and attribution. Do not alter CC BY-SA images beyond what the licence and CCC's "never altered" rule allow.

### Uncertain
**Uncertain:** The "confusion in his head" phrase is attributed to a 1774 report by Chancellor Kaunitz in secondary literature; I did not see the document. The mirror detail is widely repeated (Nicolai) but one search surfaced it only in secondary sources. The Mesmer friendship is reported in the literature but treat as "reportedly".

US: her works were published after 1930 and, as foreign works, are protected (restored under URAA), 95 years from publication: e.g. a 1961 work until 2057.**
- Rights are managed by **VEGAP** (Madrid) and in the US by **ARS (Artists Rights Society)**; credit lines read "© Remedios Varo, VEGAP" / "Artists Rights Society (ARS), New York / VEGAP, Madrid".
- **What CCC could show legally without permission:** nothing of her artwork. No PD or cleared photo of her was found.
