  our only input element; the prompt describes something happening with the theme around the head, e.g. "little bananas
  roll around his head without leaving the frame", "he takes a bite of a banana". No more editing the accessory into the
  head image (Qwen) by default.
- **Generation paused. Edit first.** All 10 videos are cut now with the material and the voice; where the head goes, the
  host's static still marks the spot (placeholder). Kling is generated only after the edit is approved, line by line,
  for the head moments that survive the edit.
